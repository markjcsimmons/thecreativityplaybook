## The big idea

Draw it, picture it, and look at the problem a different way. The chapter argues that when people have to rely on words alone, they find it hard to restructure a problem and discover new ways of looking at it.

## What's in the chapter

The pictures can be actual sketches or verbal ones, such as similes and metaphors. Either kind lets you think more creatively than a paragraph of description does.

The chapter lists techniques for visualizing a problem: sketching it out on your own, taking photos, and a group collage exercise in which people make a sketch together.

Similes and metaphors work by getting you to picture how the problem you are working on is similar to, or different from, something that at first seems unrelated. That comparison is the move behind the chapter's game.
