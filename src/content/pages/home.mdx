## Think like an artist

*The Business Playground* came out in 2010 with a simple message: think like an artist and you bring more meaning and connection to your work. Artists and musicians seem to have creativity in their DNA, and businesses have a lot to learn from how they think and make things.

*The Creativity Playbook* is the revised edition. It keeps the hands-on tools and adds a world that has changed: fewer, bigger companies, more decisions made by data, and a new kind of instrument in the creative signal chain, artificial intelligence.
