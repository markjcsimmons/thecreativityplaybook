## The big idea

Taking an idea from vision to action takes an incredible amount of perseverance and self-belief. It also helps to be surrounded by great people.

## What's in the chapter

A creative culture rewards more than success. People are allowed, and actively encouraged, to experiment and make mistakes.

The chapter lists simple ways to build that environment: keep notebooks for capturing ideas, hold idea jam sessions that show a company-wide commitment to brave ideas, embrace playfulness and risk-taking, and hire people who go against the grain and may not seem to fit.

It also looks at what motivates people to be creative at work. Extrinsic rewards include money, recognition and the expectation of being creative. Intrinsic ones include the personal challenge and intellectual curiosity. The best business playground, the authors say, is the right mix of these in a collaborative environment with a clear, shared vision.
