## The big idea

Thinking BIG can trigger interesting ideas. The chapter's tagline asks you to think big and not let the literal truth get in the way of a good story, and its star is a paperclip.

## What's in the chapter

Kyle MacDonald started with a single red paperclip. After a series of barter trades, he ended up with a three-bedroom house in Saskatchewan, Canada.

Evocative language can help you think big by dramatizing a problem and bringing it to life. It also works the other way: language can make a problem seem like hardly a problem at all.

Exaggeration is another trick. It pulls your thinking away from the more obvious solutions and lets your imagination run a little wild.
