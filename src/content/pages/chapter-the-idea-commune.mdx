## The big idea

Ideas get bigger when people build on each other. In a world where technology has broken down the walls, the chapter says, playing well with others is a necessary part of business life.

## What's in the chapter

Coming up with ideas as a group is not always easy. With the right spirit of collaboration, the results can be worth it.

Team leadership makes the difference between the good and the plain ugly. The chapter's sharpest point is that a leader's negative behaviors have far more impact than any good ones, and can knock the creative stuffing out of a team.

Good leaders know when to look for new ideas and when not to. When it is not the moment, they communicate a vision and help each person understand their role in making it happen. The book's example is Matthew Warchus, the theater and film director, one of the people the authors interviewed.
