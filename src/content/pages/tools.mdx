## Why tools, and why these

The book is blunt about brainstorms. Groups tend to avoid conflict and slide toward safe, similar ideas, and a bad brainstorm can leave everyone frustrated. Individuals are better at generating lots of different ideas, and groups are better at narrowing them down. So the tools here are built for one person or a pair first, and for a team once you have a pile of raw ideas to sort.
