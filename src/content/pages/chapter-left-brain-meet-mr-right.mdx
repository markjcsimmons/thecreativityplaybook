## The big idea

Creativity means thinking more broadly than our analytical side tends to allow. The book talks about the two halves of the brain. Neuroscientists say a strict left and right split is a myth, so we read it as a metaphor for two modes of thinking: analysis and insight.

## What's in the chapter

The most practical advice in the chapter is to make time for distractions. Going off to do something else for a while can give you better solutions than staying glued to the problem.

People switch between two ways of solving problems, analysis and the insight that arrives as an "A-ha!" moment, and often pick one without realizing it. Some people lean on insight more than others, and the book says it is likely that some people really are more creative. Everyone can think creatively, though, and certain activities bring the ability out.

Those activities include listening to music, playing video games and aerobic exercise, which the chapter says work by putting you in a good mood or increasing blood flow to the brain. There is a catch: a good mood can make you less effective at logical decisions.
