## The big idea

Half the job is choosing. Once you have made a bunch of ideas with the book's techniques, you need to work out which are worth serious time, effort and resources, and which should be killed off.

## What's in the chapter

Some companies recognize the need to move on from ideas without enough potential, and will actually pay their scientists to kill them.

This is the point where the analytical side takes over from the creative side. The chapter gives an exercise for working out which ideas are feasible and what you could do to improve them.

Gut instinct plays a big role too. Freddie DeMann, Evan Williams and Christian Audigier all told the authors that, when all is said and done, it comes down to a feeling for whether an idea is a big one. The crazy ideas you cannot find a place for now should be set aside, not thrown away, because sometimes they come back to haunt you.
