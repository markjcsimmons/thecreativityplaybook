## How to use it

1. **Save as you go.** Every tool and game has a save button: Save on the generators, and Save to notebook when a game finishes. Nothing is saved until you press it.
2. **Come back and look.** Search by word, filter by the tool an idea came from, and set each idea's stage: new, shortlisted or launched.
3. **Add your own.** Type ideas straight into the box, one per line, and edit or delete any of them.
4. **Take them with you.** Export your ideas as text or CSV, print them, or back them up to a file and restore that file on another device.

## Why keep a notebook

The book's last chapter lists keeping notebooks for capturing ideas among the simple ways to build a creative environment: if you have somewhere to put a chance idea, it is more likely to survive. The Kill the Idea chapter makes the other half of the case. Ideas that you cannot find a place for now should be set aside, not thrown away, because some of them come back.

The notebook is the place to set them aside. Save everything, worry about the best ones later, and use [Idea Spaghetti](/games/idea-spaghetti/), [Kill the Idea](/games/kill-the-idea/) and [Blast Off](/games/blast-off/) to work through them.

## Where your ideas are kept

Your ideas are stored in your own browser, on this device, and nowhere else. We never see them. That also means they do not follow you to another phone or computer. Use Back up to save a file, and Restore a backup on the other device.

Clearing your browser's site data, or using a private window, removes the notebook. If your ideas matter to you, back them up now and then.

## Questions

**Is there a limit?** The notebook holds up to 500 ideas. If it fills up, delete the ones you no longer want.

**Do I need an account?** No. There is no sign-up and no login.

**What happened to the lists I saved in the generators?** They moved into the notebook the first time you opened it, and the generators' saved lists now show what is in the notebook.

**Can I use it with other people?** Not yet. It is one person, one browser. Export a list to share it.
