## How it works

1. **Roll the dice.** The tool rolls two. If you have real dice, open "Using real dice?" and say what you rolled.
2. **Read your chapter.** An odd first die gives the chapter shown on the second die, from 1 to 6. An even first die gives the second die plus six, from 7 to 12. A pair of dice has 36 combinations and the book has 12 chapters, so each chapter comes up on three of them and none is more likely than another.
3. **Do the exercise.** You get a ten-minute version of the chapter's board game, with a timer if you want one.
4. **Keep what you made.** Most of the exercises end in ideas. Put them in your [notebook](/notebook/) with the tool that fits, or on paper.

## Where this comes from

The book's Contents page says "Life is a game and so is this book, so let's play!" and that the twelve chapters can be read in order, or not, as the mood strikes you. It adds a suggestion: try rolling a pair of dice to tell you which chapter to read next. This tool does that, then goes one step further and gives you something to do.

The book does not say how a pair of dice should point to twelve chapters, so the rule above is ours. The exercises are ours too. Each is a shortened version of the game at the end of its chapter, cut to ten minutes, and each says what the book's own version asks for.

## Questions

**What is Chapter Dice?** A tool that picks one of the book's twelve chapters with two dice and gives you a ten-minute exercise from it.

**Is the dice rule from the book?** The idea of rolling a pair of dice to choose a chapter is the book's. The rule that maps the dice to chapters is ours.

**Are the exercises the book's games?** They are shortened versions of the games, written by us to fit ten minutes. Each one says how the book's own version differs.

**Can I use real dice?** Yes. Open "Using real dice?" under the roll button and choose the two numbers.

**Do I need other people?** Most exercises work alone. Chapter 7's brainwriting game needs four or more people, so roll again if you are on your own.
