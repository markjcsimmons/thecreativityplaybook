# Editing audit: The Creativity Playbook (V2 comp)

Run 2026-11-22 on the V2 comp (266 PDF pages, about 70,000 words). The full list is in `editing-audit.csv` (open in Excel or Numbers). Fill in the **Decision** column (approve / reject / change) and I'll apply approved items to the manuscript.

**103 items:** 28 must-fix (P1), 43 should-fix (P2), 32 optional or style (P3).

## What the audit found

- **Rights:** the SENZ photo, the *Being There* excerpt and the HBR "Weird Rules" extract are still in the book; the Pearson ad and Pearson thanks are still there; the Dylan and Stones clearances need checking for ebook and the new edition.
- **The book's own web address is gone:** `www.businessplayground.com` is printed 5 times and now redirects to a domain sale page.
- **People and companies that have changed:** Twitter is now X; Nokia's phone business was sold in 2013; Paul Allen (2018), Steve Jobs (2011), Sir Ken Robinson (2020), Jack Welch (2020) and Christian Audigier (2015) have died but are written about in the present tense; Tim Brown stepped down as IDEO CEO in 2019; 3M's CEO changed in 2012; Better Place went bankrupt in 2013; Club Penguin (2017) and StumbleUpon (2018) closed.
- **Dated claims:** 2008 IBM and BCG surveys, "recent" studies and "nowadays" claims, relative dates like "a few years ago". The book cites dozens of studies and articles from 2003 to 2009.
- **Quotes with weak attribution:** Twain (not his), Franklin, Picasso, Henry Ford.
- **Left/right brain:** 42 mentions, 16 in Chapter 5, presented as science.
- **Dead or changed links:** of 21 addresses checked, 3 do not respond, 2 return 404 and 3 redirect to different sites (one is the book's own domain). Details are in the CSV.
- **Other:** Chapter 9's cards name living public figures; "Dead PDA day" is baked into the board art; the cover has a typo; British spelling (203 tokens).

## How I did it, and what I did not do

- I extracted the text page by page and ran pattern scans for time words, years, role claims, statistics, URLs, quotes, brain claims, sensitive terms, credits and title mentions. I then read each flagged passage.
- I checked 21 web addresses by script and ran about 18 web searches to confirm the status of people, companies and quotes. Items I could not confirm are marked **verify** or **Not verified**.
- I did **not** read all 70,000 words end to end, so a dated claim with no tell-tale words could be missing. I did not audit each Index entry (they will need updating after edits). The current status of Danny Socolof, Ethan Imboden, Freddie DeMann, Jill Dumain, Tero Ojanperä, Philip Hess and Kyle MacDonald is unknown; the authors would know.
