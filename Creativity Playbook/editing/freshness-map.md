# Freshness map: The Creativity Playbook (draft for Mark, 2026-11-22)

Purpose: set how hard each chapter gets edited before I write any more edit briefs. Nothing here has been applied. Chapter 1 is the pilot and is done (36 items, 30 approved and applied, 6 questions answered).

**How to read it.** Each chapter gets one level:
- **Light** = line edits, fix dated wording and links, a couple of sourced additions, 1-2 tools. About +10%.
- **Replace** = swap dated or rights-risky examples, stats and studies for current ones. Length stays about the same, but a quarter to a half of the chapter's text changes.
- **Rebuild** = the chapter's evidence base is out of date enough that it is rewritten around current research, keeping Dave's stories and the chapter's idea.
- **New** = material added that the 2010 book could not have had (AI, remote and hybrid work).

**Caveats.** Levels come from the audit (103 items) plus each chapter's theme. I did not read all 70,000 words end to end, so a chapter can move up a level once I read it closely. The "text touched" figures are planning guesses, not measurements. Page counts are from the V2 contents page (book pages). Audit counts are by the chapter where the item starts, and several items (Evan Williams, businessplayground.com, Nokia) recur across chapters.

## What this adds up to

- **Length.** Most of the change is swaps, so length stays near +10-15%. The new AI and remote-work material (Introduction, Chapters 2, 7, 10, 11, 12, Conclusion) could take the total to about +20%. You can cap it by making the AI content a single new section instead of threading it through chapters.
- **Biggest jobs, in order:** Chapter 5 (rebuild), Introduction (replace and AI), Chapter 12 (replace), Chapter 3 (replace), then Chapters 9, 10 and 11.
- **Rights-driven cuts that need replacement text regardless of ambition level:** SENZ (Ch 3), Being There (Ch 6), HBR Weird Rules (Ch 12).
- **Dave's decision needed before I touch them:** Hospital Club and Paul Allen (Ch 7), Sponsorbility (Ch 3), Villaraigosa and the drug story (Chs 1 and 9), the 2027 AI introduction.

## What I would not touch
Dave's stories and voice, the chapter order and the metaphor set (spaghetti, scavengers, blast off), the board game, and the design.

## Decisions for you
1. **Ambition per chapter.** Accept the levels above or change them (the main choice is Chapter 5: rebuild or keep the title and soften the science claims).
2. **AI content:** one new section, or woven through several chapters.
3. **Next batch.** My suggestion is Chapter 2 (the lightest, tests the Light + New recipe) and Chapter 5 (the heaviest, tests Rebuild). That gives a range before doing the rest.
4. **Style of new first-person material.** I cannot write Dave's or Mark's anecdotes. Where a swap needs one, I will leave a marked gap for you to fill.
